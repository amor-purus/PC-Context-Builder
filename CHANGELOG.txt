AI PC Intelligence v1.0.7
- Removed transient() and grab_set() from DriveSelection and ReviewWindow to avoid Windows Tk parent/child activation deadlocks and taskbar restore failures.
- Made DriveSelection and ReviewWindow independent modeless native windows. The main scan button is disabled while a drive selector or review window is open, preventing duplicate scans without an application grab.
- Added a one-time lift() and focus_force() request after App, DriveSelection, and ReviewWindow initialization. Focus is not repeatedly stolen during normal operation.
- Added explicit parent= to every messagebox call so confirmation, warning, and error dialogs stay logically attached to the owning application/dialog window.
- Added source-level regression checks for forbidden transient/grab calls, parent-bound messageboxes, focus activation, and duplicate-scan prevention.
