- HiDPI mitigation candidate added in `main.py`: on Windows, call `shcore.SetProcessDpiAwareness(1)` (system DPI-aware) before `Tk()` creates the first window. This follows CPython IDLE's established `fix_win_hidpi` approach and is intended to prevent bitmap stretching of a DPI-unaware Tk window at the system display scale. If Windows reports that process awareness is already set, or the API is unavailable, the app preserves the existing mode and continues startup.
- Regression guards assert that the DPI-awareness call precedes `Tk()`; on Windows, the built-in self-test also queries `GetProcessDpiAwareness` and rejects a still-unaware process.
